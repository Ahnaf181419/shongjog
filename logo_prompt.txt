## Shongjog Logo Generation Prompt

Generate a logo for "Shongjog" (সংযোগ), a Bangladeshi emergency companion mobile app.

### App Identity

- Name meaning: "Shongjog" (সংযোগ) means "connection/link" in Bangla — connecting people
  to safety when disaster strikes.
- Tagline concept: "Works when the internet doesn't"
- Purpose: Offline-first, AI-powered emergency guidance app for floods and cyclones in
  Bangladesh. Delivers first-aid, shelter finding, emergency calling, and peer-to-peer
  mesh communication — all without internet.

### Visual Symbolism to Convey

1. Connection/Link — the literal meaning of "Shongjog"; mesh networking between people
2. Safety/Shelter — a protective canopy, umbrella, or shelter shape
3. Bangladesh identity — subtle nod to Bangladeshi culture (water, river delta, green/red
   national colors woven in naturally, not as a flag)
4. Voice/Speech — the app is voice-first (microphone concept, sound waves)
5. AI intelligence — subtle neural/brain or signal motif, not literal robot
6. Resilience — strength, reliability, calm authority in crisis

### Style Direction

- Feel: Calm-in-crisis, authoritative, premium, trustworthy — NOT playful or cute
- Genre: Modern tech app icon / logo mark, similar tier to WhatsApp, Telegram, Google
  Meet in simplicity and memorability
- Shape: Must work as a rounded square app icon (iOS/Android adaptive icon), a circular
  favicon, and a horizontal lockup (icon + wordmark)
- Simplicity: The mark must read clearly at 48x48 px (notification icon) and scale up
  to hero size
- Avoid: Medical crosses (conflicts with emergency, too literal), generic AI brain
  clichés, literal phone illustrations, overly complex illustrations

### Color Palette (from the app's design system — "Deep Ocean Blue")

- Primary: #0369A1 (Deep Ocean Blue, sky-700) — the locked brand hue (~205°)
- Bright accent: #38BDF8 (sky-400) — for dark backgrounds
- Alert/Emergency (accent only): #DC2626 (red-600) — used sparingly, never decorative
- Surface: #F8FAFC (slate-50) light / #0F172A (slate-900) dark
- Neutrals: Slate scale for text — #0F172A to #94A3B8
- The logo should work in monochrome white on ocean blue, monochrome ocean on white,
  and full-color

### Typography Context

- The app's UI font is HindSiliguri (a Bangla-first sans-serif). If the wordmark
  includes Bangla script (সংযোগ), it should feel cohesive with this typeface's weight
  and rhythm — clean, rounded terminals, moderate stroke contrast.
- The English wordmark "Shongjog" should use a clean geometric sans-serif (think Inter,
  Plus Jakarta Sans, or similar).

### Composition Options to Explore

1. Abstract mark only (symbol, no text) — for the app icon
2. Symbol + English wordmark — for marketing headers
3. Symbol + Bangla wordmark (সংযোগ) — for localized materials
4. Horizontal lockup — icon left, text right

### Detailed Symbol Concepts (explore 2–3 of these)

- Concept A — "Convergence Shield": Two flowing lines (representing rivers/people)
  converging into a single protective arc or shelter canopy shape. The convergence point
  forms a subtle speech bubble or signal node. The overall silhouette reads as a shield —
  safety.

- Concept B — "Mesh Ripple": A central node with 3–4 connected nodes in a mesh pattern,
  enclosed in a soft circle. The nodes could subtly evoke a Bangladeshi water lily
  (shapla) arrangement. Conveys peer-to-peer connection and community resilience.

- Concept C — "Voice Wave Shelter": A stylized shelter/roof shape formed by concentric
  sound/voice waves. The negative space between waves creates the silhouette of a
  protective structure. Conveys voice-first + shelter/safety.

- Concept D — "Delta Signal": A geometric representation of a river delta (Bangladesh =
  land of rivers) with signal/connection dots at each tributary endpoint. Clean, abstract,
  premium.

### Technical Requirements

- Vector (SVG/AI) output, pixel-perfect at all sizes
- App icon: 1024x1024 master, works at 48px
- Favicon: circular crop friendly
- Must have strong contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum) against both light and dark
  backgrounds
- No thin hairlines that disappear at small sizes — minimum stroke weight proportional
  to icon size
- Flat/modern design, minimal gradients (if any, subtle depth only)
- No drop shadows in the mark itself

### Mood References

- The trustworthiness of 112 Emergency apps
- The simplicity and memorability of Telegram's paper plane
- The calm authority of WHO's visual identity
- The tech-forward feel of Google's Material You iconography
- Bangladesh's natural beauty: river deltas, green landscapes, monsoon resilience

### What This Logo Must Communicate (priority order)

1. Safety & Trust — "This app will help me in an emergency"
2. Connection — "It connects me to help, to people, to guidance"
3. Technology — "This is intelligent, modern, and reliable"
4. Bangladesh — "This is made for me, for my country"
