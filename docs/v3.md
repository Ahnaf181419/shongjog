# AI Disaster Copilot (Project Plan)

**Hackathon Theme:** AI for Disaster Management
**Primary AI Model:** Gemma E2B/E4B
**Frontend:** Flutter
**Backend:** FastAPI (optional, lightweight)
**Database:** Firebase Firestore / Supabase
**Maps:** OpenStreetMap + Flutter Map
**Target Platform:** Android (offline-first)

---

# 1. Vision

Build an **AI-first disaster management assistant** where every major feature is powered by Gemma rather than relying on static rules or forms.

The application helps citizens prepare for disasters, report incidents, receive personalized guidance, and enables emergency responders to receive structured, AI-processed information.

Unlike traditional disaster apps, AI is integrated into every workflow—not just as a chatbot.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

During floods, cyclones, earthquakes, and fires, people face several problems:

* They don't know what to do.
* Emergency reports are often incomplete.
* Authorities receive duplicate or unclear information.
* Citizens panic and struggle to communicate.
* Personalized preparedness is lacking.
* Disaster information is difficult to understand.

Current disaster apps mostly provide:

* News
* Static checklists
* Emergency phone numbers

They rarely leverage AI to assist users in real time.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

Create an AI-powered mobile application that provides:

* Personalized disaster planning
* AI-powered incident reporting
* Image understanding
* Voice understanding
* Dynamic emergency guidance
* AI-generated summaries
* AI risk assessment

Every important interaction invokes Gemma.

# 12. Demo Scenario

1. User opens the app.
2. AI greets the user and loads the family profile.
3. User photographs a flooded street.
4. AI identifies flooding and estimates severity.
5. User records a voice message describing trapped neighbors.
6. AI converts the recording into a structured incident report.
7. User reviews and submits the report.
8. AI updates a local situation summary.
9. User asks, "What should we do before evacuating?"
10. AI provides personalized guidance based on the family profile.

This sequence demonstrates multiple AI capabilities working together without relying solely on a chat interface.

# Why this project is competitive

Rather than presenting AI as an optional chatbot, the application embeds Gemma into each primary workflow. Users interact through purpose-built interfaces—camera, voice, planners, reports, and personalized guidance—while AI performs reasoning, extraction, summarization, and recommendations behind the scenes. This produces a more cohesive product and demonstrates practical AI integration across the entire mobile experience.
